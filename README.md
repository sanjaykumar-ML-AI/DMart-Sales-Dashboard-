# DMart Revenue Growth Analysis Dashboard
DMart Sales Dashboard using Looker Studio Business Intelligence Tool 

**Problem Statement**

Businesses often struggle to track sales performance, customer trends, regional growth, and profitability across multiple locations. The objective of this dashboard was to create a centralized sales analytics solution for monitoring KPIs and improving business decision-making.

**Project Summary**

Developed an interactive Looker Studio Sales Dashboard to analyze sales, profit, orders, customers, products, and regional performance. The dashboard provides dynamic visualizations, trend analysis, and business insights for efficient sales monitoring.

**Dashboard Impact on Business**

The dashboard enabled real-time visibility into sales and profit performance across regions, helping businesses identify high-performing markets and growth opportunities. It improved decision-making by tracking customer behavior, order trends, and shipping performance through interactive KPIs and filters.

By analyzing quarterly sales growth and regional contributions, the business could optimize sales strategies, improve operational planning, and focus on profitable regions and products. The dashboard also simplified reporting processes and enhanced overall business transparency.

**Key Business Recommendations**

*Focus more on high-performing regions like West and East for revenue growth

*Improve sales strategies in lower-performing regions to balance business performance

*Optimize shipping methods to improve customer delivery experience

*Increase focus on high-demand products and customer retention strategies

*Use sales trend analysis for better forecasting and inventory planning

**Learning Outcomes**

*Gained practical experience in building interactive dashboards using Looker Studio

*Improved skills in KPI reporting, sales analytics, and data storytelling

*Learned to analyze customer, regional, and sales performance trends effectively

*Enhanced understanding of business intelligence and dashboard design principles

*Developed expertise in transforming raw sales data into actionable insights
